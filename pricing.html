Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Community edition include all features?

Yes! The Community edition includes all 188 HR modules with full functionality. The difference between Community and Pro is deployment method, cloud infrastructure, and support level. You get the complete HCM platform with unlimited users at no cost.

What is included in Pro support?

Pro includes priority email support with 24-hour response time, community forum access, and comprehensive documentation. Enterprise customers get phone and chat support with dedicated account managers.

Can I switch plans or upgrade later?

Absolutely! You can start with Community and upgrade to Pro at any time. All your data will be migrated seamlessly to the cloud. Similarly, you can downgrade from Pro to Community if needed. There are no lock-in contracts or switching penalties.

What are the AGPL license implications?

The Community edition is licensed under AGPL v3, which means if you modify the code and use it internally, you must share those modifications with your users. Most organizations use the software as-is, which has no restrictions. If you need a different license, contact our sales team for a commercial license option.

How does Pro pricing work?

Pro pricing is per active user per month. Monthly billing is $12 per user, and annual billing is $9 per user (a 25% discount). You only pay for users who actively use the system. There are no setup fees or hidden charges, and you can add or remove users anytime.

What compliance certifications does Wana.Work have?

Pro and Enterprise plans include GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2 Type II compliance. We also provide compliance reports and audit trails. For Community users, you have full control of your deployment and can implement your own security measures.

Can I import data from other HCM systems?

Yes! We support data import from most popular HCM systems including Workday, BambooHR, ADP, and Gusto. Our team provides migration assistance for Pro and Enterprise customers at no additional cost. The process typically takes 2-4 weeks depending on data complexity.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

Yes! Pro includes a 14-day free trial with full functionality and priority support. No credit card required. After the trial, you can upgrade to a paid plan or downgrade to Community at any time.

Our Mission

We believe work should be a place people actually want to be.

Where people want to work.

HR software has been built for decades around the needs of Fortune 500 companies and their budgets. We asked a different question: what if it was built for everyone?

Wana.Work exists to democratize enterprise HR software by making it open source, beautiful, and accessible to every organization regardless of size. We believe that every company, from startups to enterprises, deserves access to world class Human Capital Management tools. We believe HR software should be transparent, customizable, and truly belong to the people who use it every day.

Our Journey

Key milestones in building where people want to work

2020

Vision Conceived

The open source HCM vision takes shape as founders recognize the need for better alternatives to proprietary enterprise software.

2021

Core Platform Development

Building the foundation with modular architecture, APIs, and commitment to open source principles under AGPL v3.

2022

Public Launch

First public release brings the open source HCM platform to developers and organizations worldwide.

2023

100 Modules Milestone

Reaching 100 feature modules across recruitment, payroll, benefits, compliance, and more.

2024

Community Reaches 150+ Contributors

A global community of developers, HR professionals, and enthusiasts collaborating on the future of HR software.

2025

Wana.Work Brand Launch

Introducing Wana.Work as the unified brand for our mission to transform enterprise HR software.

Our Core Values

Guiding principles that shape everything we build

🔓

Open by Default

Transparency in our code and communication. Every decision, every line of code, and every roadmap item is visible and open to community input.

👥

People First

Every feature decision starts with the user. We listen to HR professionals, employees, and customers to understand their real needs and pain points.

💰

Enterprise Without the Tax

Powerful enterprise capabilities without the enterprise pricing. Affordable for small teams, scalable for large organizations, and fair for everyone in between.

🌍

Community Driven

Our roadmap is shaped by our contributors and users. Decisions about features, priorities, and direction come from the community that uses the platform every day.

✨

Beautiful by Design

HR software doesn't have to be ugly. We invest in design, user experience, and accessibility. Beautiful software is more usable and more enjoyable.

🔒

Data Belongs to You

Your data is your data. Full privacy, data sovereignty, and control. No vendor lock in, no hidden data collection, no surprises.

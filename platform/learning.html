Week 1 Essentials

Complete the following during your first week to ensure a smooth transition and integration with the team. Your manager will be available to support you throughout this process. Don't hesitate to reach out with any questions.

Account and Access Setup

Ensure all required accounts and systems access have been provisioned. This includes email, collaboration tools, documentation portals, and project management platforms specific to your role.

Team Introduction

Schedule individual meetings with your direct team members and stakeholders. These conversations will help you understand team dynamics, current projects, and your role in the organization.

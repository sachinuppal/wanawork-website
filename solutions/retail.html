A Day in the Life: Multi-Store Retail Manager

David manages HR for a 25-store retail chain during the busy holiday season with 200 full-time and 300 seasonal employees.

His morning starts with the Recruitment dashboard. He sees that 250 applications came in overnight for seasonal positions. He quickly screens, ranks, and sends offers to top candidates. By noon, 50 new hires have been added to the system.

A store manager reports low staffing for the weekend. David uses WorkSchedules to see real-time coverage across all locations and identifies stores that can shift workers. He initiates the swap and sends notifications to affected employees.

"With Wana.Work, I went from spending 40 hours per week on hiring and scheduling to maybe 10. We're faster, more compliant, and our employee satisfaction has actually improved because schedules are transparent."

By afternoon, he's reviewing time entries and overtime reports, ensuring all labor law compliance, then reviewing new hire satisfaction surveys.

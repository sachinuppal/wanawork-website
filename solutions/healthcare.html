A Day in the Life: Community Hospital HR

Sarah, the HR Manager at Community General Hospital, starts her day checking Wana.Work.

She reviews the credential alert dashboard and sees that 3 RNs need license renewal within 30 days. She sends automated renewal reminders through the system.

A nurse calls in sick for the afternoon shift. Sarah uses WorkSchedules to identify available coverage and facilitate a shift swap with another nurse. The system handles notifications and pay adjustments.

"With Wana.Work, I've reduced credential management time by 70% and our scheduling is 100% compliant. I actually have time to focus on culture and retention."

By afternoon, Sarah is using the performance module to prepare for a 6-month clinical review with one of her new hires. Everything is documented, compliant, and professional.

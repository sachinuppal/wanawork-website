A Day in the Life: Department of HR at State University

Lisa manages HR for a mid-sized state university with 200 faculty and 150 staff across multiple departments.

She starts her semester by setting up academic calendars in Wana.Work and scheduling around semester dates, finals weeks, and institutional events. Part-time and adjunct faculty have different scheduling needs, which the system handles automatically.

A tenure review is coming up. Lisa pulls up the tenure tracking dashboard to see which faculty are in their year 6 review. She notes their documentation progress and schedules the appraisal meetings.

"Wana.Work's academic calendar integration has been transformative. We no longer struggle with scheduling conflicts, and tenure tracking is finally transparent and systematic."

By afternoon, she's registering faculty for summer professional development conferences and tracking their attendance. Everything is centralized and accreditation-ready.

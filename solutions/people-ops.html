A day in the life of a People Ops specialist

Morning
Process and Automate
New hire paperwork flows in automatically. Onboarding workflow triggers, welcome emails sent, IT provisioning initiated. Check your workflow dashboard and spot any bottlenecks.
Midday
Approve and Manage
Review leave requests through the approval workflow. Update employee records, watch changes cascade through the system. Self-service portal handles questions you'd normally answer 50 times a day.
Afternoon
Report and Prepare
Generate automated reports on hiring progress and employee lifecycle metrics. Plan next week's onboarding schedule and set up training workflows for new hires.

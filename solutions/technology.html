A Day in the Life: Head of People Ops

Maya manages HR for a growing tech company that just closed Series B. Her team is now 150 engineers across SF, Austin, and London.

She starts her day by reviewing the Wana.Work dashboard for hiring pipeline updates. She sees 12 new candidates in the first round and approves moving 3 through to the next stage. Offer letters are auto-generated for last week's accepted offers.

An engineer asks about career progression. Maya uses the Competencies module to show the clear IC1 through principal engineer path and identifies a personalized skill development plan.

"Wana.Work's API-first design has saved us countless engineering hours. We integrated it with our own tools, and our entire hiring process is now streamlined. Culture and growth are finally scaling with the company."

By afternoon, she's running a retrospective on retention metrics and using performance data to identify high performers for senior promotions.

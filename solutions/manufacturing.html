A Day in the Life: Plant Operations Manager

James manages HR across two manufacturing plants with over 300 employees and rotating shifts.

His morning starts by reviewing the Wana.Work dashboard for multi-plant staffing levels. He checks that night shift staffing is adequate and verifies all CNC operators have current certifications.

A new OSHA training requirement is released. James uses the Trainings module to create a mandatory course, assign it to all affected employees, and track completion status across both plants.

"With Wana.Work managing our multi-site operations, I have full compliance visibility and zero scheduling conflicts. Our safety metrics have improved significantly."

By mid-afternoon, James is coordinating a shift swap request and reviewing incident reports. Everything is documented and audit-ready within minutes.
